Interests & Involvement

What I’m into,
on and off the clock.

The experience page covers what I’ve already done. This one is about where I want all of that to go, the groups I’m a part of along the way, and what I end up doing when nobody is paying me or grading me.

01 · Professional Interests

Where I want to end up.

I want to be an entrepreneur and a business owner, and I want to do it in the web design industry. Everything else on this list is a field that sits close to that, and I’d like to keep working in all of them as my career goes on.

  • 01

    Entrepreneurship & Business Ownership

    This is the main one. I want to own the business I work for, and I would say co owning a small studio while I’m still in school is my way of finding out early whether I’m any good at it. Going forward I want to grow that into a real company with its own clients and, eventually, its own employees.

  • 02

    The Web Design Industry

    Web design is the industry I want to build that business in. I like that a website is both a design problem and a technical one, and I think there will always be small companies that need a good one and don’t know where to start, so it’s the field I plan on staying in for a long time.

  • 03

    Business Software & Information Systems

    A website is usually the first thing a client asks for and not the last, so I want to keep moving into the software behind it, like customer management systems and reporting tools. Taking Management Information Systems this semester has made me a lot more interested in that side of the industry.

  • 04

    Digital Marketing & Analytics

    A site that nobody finds doesn’t help anybody, so I want to be able to offer search, paid ads, and analytics along with the design. That’s the reason I earned the Google Ads and Google Analytics certifications, and I plan on adding more of them.

  • 05

    Video & Visual Storytelling

    Video is how I got into any of this in the first place, and I don’t want to leave it behind. I’d like the business to be able to shoot the footage for a client as well as build the site it goes on, since those two things usually get hired out separately.

  • 06

    AI & Automation

    I think this is where the industry is headed, and I want to be ahead of it instead of catching up to it. I’m most interested in finding the places where automation actually saves a small business an hour, and then building that into what I offer.

The goal all of this points at

Long term I want to own and run a web design company that builds websites and software for small businesses that can’t afford an agency. I’m not super experienced at leading people yet, and I know I have a lot to learn about the business side, but that’s the career I’m working toward and it’s the reason I’m taking business courses on top of my design ones.

03 · Hobbies & Personal Interests

The person behind the resume.

Most of what I do for fun isn’t that far from what I do for work, and I think that says something about me, but I do have a few things that have nothing to do with a screen.

  • Piano

    I play piano, and it’s the one creative thing I do that nobody is waiting on. I would say it has taught me patience more than anything, since you can’t really rush a piece into sounding good, and that carries over to design work more than I expected it to.

  • Basketball

    I play pickup whenever I can find a game and I follow both college and pro ball. Pickup means playing with whoever shows up, so you figure out pretty quickly how to work with people you met five minutes ago.

  • Umpiring

    I’ve umpired youth travel baseball since 2022. It started as a way to make some money and it turned into the best practice I’ve had at making a call under pressure and staying calm when a coach doesn’t agree with it.

  • Spanish

    I took a few years of Spanish in high school and I’m taking it again at Southern Nazarene. I’m not fluent, but I can follow a lot more of a conversation than I used to, and I think it’s made me a better listener in general.

  • Photography

    I take way more photos than I ever end up using. Shooting for fun is how I practice framing and light without a client involved, and it has helped my attention to detail on the paid work.

  • Gaming & Side Projects

    I play sports video games with my friends, and I also build things nobody asked for just to see if I can. Those two overlapped when I built a site that keeps track of our wins and losses, and I rebuilt a block puzzle game I’ve played since eighth grade. Projects like that are how I learn most of the skills I end up using at work.
