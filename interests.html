Interests & Involvement

What I’m into,
on and off the clock.

The experience page covers what I’ve already done. This one is about where I want all of that to go, the groups I’m a part of along the way, and what I end up doing when nobody is paying me or grading me.

01 · Professional Interests

Where I want to end up.

My professional interests are web design, video, business software, digital marketing, and running a company of my own. I’d say what ties them together is that I’m interested in how a small business can use design and technology to actually get more customers and waste less time.

  • 01

    Web Design & Development

    This is the one I spend the most time on. I like that a website is both a design problem and a technical one, and I don’t think I’d be happy only doing one half of it. My design courses gave me the layout and type side, and building real sites for an employer and for clients has taught me the code side.

  • 02

    Video & Visual Storytelling

    Video is how I got into any of this in the first place, and it’s still what I do on campus every week for the student news show. I’m interested in where video and the web overlap, since most businesses have a lot of good footage and pretty much nowhere useful to put it.

  • 03

    Information Systems for Small Business

    I didn’t expect to care about this one, but I’ve ended up building customer management systems and reporting tools for the companies I work with, and I really like it. Taking Management Information Systems this semester has given me the vocabulary for work I was already doing, which is a pretty nice way to take a class.

  • 04

    Digital Marketing & Analytics

    A site that nobody finds doesn’t help anybody, so I’ve gotten interested in local search, paid ads, and reading the analytics afterward to see if any of it worked. That’s the reason I went and earned the Google Ads and Google Analytics certifications.

  • 05

    AI & Automation

    I use AI tools in my work every day, and I think the interesting question isn’t whether a business should use them but where they actually save somebody an hour. I’ve automated reports that used to get put together by hand, and I’d like to keep getting better at spotting that kind of opportunity.

  • 06

    Entrepreneurship & Leadership

    Co owning a studio has made me care about pricing, contracts, and client relationships about as much as the work itself. I’m not super experienced at leading people yet, but I’d like to end up running a small team, and the business courses I’m taking are a big part of how I’m getting ready for that.

The goal all of this points at

In the short term I’m interested in roles where I’m responsible for a company’s whole digital presence, something like a marketing and web specialist or a digital media manager. Long term I want to keep growing a studio that builds websites and internal software for small businesses that can’t afford an agency, because that’s the work I’ve enjoyed the most so far.

03 · Hobbies & Personal Interests

The person behind the resume.

Most of what I do for fun isn’t that far from what I do for work, and I think that says something about me, but I do have a few things that have nothing to do with a screen.

  • Piano

    I play piano, and it’s the one creative thing I do that nobody is waiting on. I would say it has taught me patience more than anything, since you can’t really rush a piece into sounding good, and that carries over to design work more than I expected it to.

  • Basketball

    I play pickup whenever I can find a game and I follow both college and pro ball. Pickup means playing with whoever shows up, so you figure out pretty quickly how to work with people you met five minutes ago.

  • Umpiring

    I’ve umpired youth travel baseball since 2022. It started as a way to make some money and it turned into the best practice I’ve had at making a call under pressure and staying calm when a coach doesn’t agree with it.

  • Spanish

    I took a few years of Spanish in high school and I’m taking it again at Southern Nazarene. I’m not fluent, but I can follow a lot more of a conversation than I used to, and I think it’s made me a better listener in general.

  • Photography

    I take way more photos than I ever end up using. Shooting for fun is how I practice framing and light without a client involved, and it has helped my attention to detail on the paid work.

  • Gaming & Side Projects

    I play sports video games with my friends, and I also build things nobody asked for just to see if I can. Those two overlapped when I built a site that keeps track of our wins and losses, and I rebuilt a block puzzle game I’ve played since eighth grade. Projects like that are how I learn most of the skills I end up using at work.
